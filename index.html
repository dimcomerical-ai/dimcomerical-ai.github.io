TR Domain Scout

Keyword planning and domain research for Türkiye.

TR Domain Scout helps its operator request aggregated keyword-planning metrics from the Google Ads API, review Turkish-language keyword demand, check selected domain extensions through public RDAP services, and export research results to a local CSV file.

Purpose

A focused research pipeline

TR Domain Scout supports pre-campaign keyword research and domain-name research. It does not create advertisements, modify Google Ads campaigns, manage billing, register domains, or provide services to the public.

01

Collect candidates

Review operator-provided terms and selected public sources to assemble candidate keywords.

02

Measure demand

Request aggregated keyword metrics for Türkiye from authorized search-planning services.

03

Check domains

Query public RDAP services for selected extensions and export the findings for manual review.

Google API use

Limited to keyword-planning research

The application uses the Google Ads API only for authorized keyword-planning requests made by its operator. It does not request Google profile, Gmail, Contacts, Drive, Photos, or other personal Google-account content.

Data requested

Aggregated keyword ideas, historical search metrics, competition indicators, and the account identifiers required to submit the request.

How data is used

Results are displayed to the operator for research and may be exported locally as CSV. They are not sold or used to profile other people.

No automated advertising

TR Domain Scout does not create or modify campaigns, ads, audiences, budgets, billing profiles, users, or payment methods.

No prohibited imagery

The application does not generate, upload, distribute, or facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery or any other intimate imagery.

Data handling

Minimal access, transparent use

No public user accounts

The application is not offered as a public SaaS product and does not provide user registration.

Service-account authentication

Google Ads API access is performed by a dedicated service account authorized by the operator.

No advertising activity

The application does not create campaigns, ads, budgets, billing profiles, or payment transactions.

Operator-controlled exports

Research results are exported only when the operator requests a local CSV download.

Important

Research results require independent verification.

Search-volume figures are estimates, not website traffic. An RDAP result is not a reservation or a guarantee that a domain can be registered. The operator must confirm availability with a registrar.
